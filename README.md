# neural-parliament
# Project PARLIAMENT: A Self-Governing Neural Architecture

**This repository contains the proof-of-concept for a new paradigm in AI design: a neural network structured not as a monolithic black box, but as a constitutional government with a clear separation of powers.**

We challenged a fundamental dogma of AI: What if we could build an AI that governs itself, polices its own internal components, and makes decisions based on a rational, hierarchical process?

This is the result: a working prototype of a neural mind with three distinct branches of power.

---

## The Architecture: A Digital Separation of Powers

Our Parliament is a modular system where different neural components are assigned specific roles, creating a system of checks and balances.

*   **The Executive Branch (The Coordinator):** Issues directives (`control_signals`) to specialized "ministries" and, crucially, **listens to feedback** to update its strategy in real-time.
*   **The Judicial Branch (The Feedback System):** A specialized ministry submits a "report" (`decision`) that influences the Executive's next actions, ensuring decisions are deliberate.
*   **The Internal Security Service (The Circuit Breakers):** Each ministry is monitored by its own autonomous `NeuralCircuitBreaker`. If a ministry is internally compromised, its own security unit instantly detects the failure and raises an alarm.

---

## The Proof: Simulating a Ministerial Crisis

We put our Parliament to the ultimate test by silently sabotaging one of its key ministries. The result was a complete success.

*   **Healthy State:** The system functions perfectly, and all Circuit Breakers report `HEALTHY`.
*   **Sabotaged State:** The system's output is flawed, **but** the sabotaged ministry's own Circuit Breaker instantly detects the internal treason and reports its status as **`BROKEN`**.

**Conclusion:** This demonstrates the viability of a self-regulating, self-policing neural government. It is an AI that knows when a part of itself has become unreliable.
